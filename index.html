Understanding Stocking Limits

This tool provides a simplified bioload estimation. It is a starting point, not a perfect science. Stocking success depends heavily on filtration strength and your maintenance schedule.

The Two Golden Rules

  • Filtration is King: An overstocked tank with a powerful canister filter and weekly water changes can succeed, while a lightly stocked tank with poor filtration and low maintenance can fail.
  • Observation: Always prioritize observing your fish for signs of stress or aggression over any general guideline.

Why These Numbers Matter

Overstocking leads to rapid nitrate and ammonia buildup, stunted growth, increased stress, and disease. It's always better to start conservatively.
